Albert's Imagebook — exact image export (Node 22+, ImageMagick 7)

Plan the artwork panel BEFORE generating it:
  node scripts/compose.mjs --plan --layout top-bottom --preset portrait

Compose original above artwork, each exactly half of a 1080x1440 PNG:
  node scripts/compose.mjs --source photo.jpg --art art.png --out comparison.png \
    --layout top-bottom --preset portrait

Export the artwork by itself:
  node scripts/compose.mjs --art art.png --out artwork.png --preset portrait

Layouts: design-only (default), top-bottom, left-right.
Presets: portrait (default), feed, square, story, landscape.
Custom dimensions: --size 1600x1200 instead of --preset.
Original: --photo-fit cover (default) fills its half without padding, cropping excess only.
          --photo-fit contain keeps the whole photo with padding when explicitly requested.
          --photo-gravity center (default) chooses the crop/placement anchor.
          Other anchors: north, northeast, east, southeast, south, southwest, west, northwest.
Inspect the crop for important subjects; e.g. --photo-gravity west retains a left-edge subject.
Artwork must match the planned panel aspect; generate another composition for other aspects.
Up to two pixels of model rounding are normalized; larger aspect mismatches are rejected.
Original files and existing outputs are never overwritten. PNG output only.

Breathing space and natural edges are composed by the image model inside the artwork.
The exporter preserves the complete image; it adds no mat, crop, or feather mask to the artwork.
  --background '#f6f3ed'  padding for photo-fit contain only (default warm white)

The plan reports the final canvas and panel dimensions.
Supply the complete generated artwork, including its paper/background, to --art.
