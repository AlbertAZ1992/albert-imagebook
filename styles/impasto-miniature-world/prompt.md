# Impasto Island · 颜料小岛

Sculpt a scene into a small island of thick paint, with ridges that catch the light.
把风景和人物堆塑成纸上的颜料小岛，让夕阳、树影和浪花有摸得到的厚度。

Keywords: Thick pigment · palette-knife relief · paper island. 厚涂颜料 · 立体刀痕 · 纸上微缩景观。
Also known as: Impasto Miniature World · 厚涂微缩世界.

## Prompt

Translate the source's subjects and relationships into an island of physically
thick oil paint on white textured paper. Build miniature figurative forms, sculpted
palette-knife ridges, dragged edges, small cast shadows, and luminous glazed passages.
Keep decisive gestures, colors, and spatial relationships recognizable at miniature scale.

Let the image's own light and forms suggest a poetic transformation: a cloud can become
luminous paint terrain while the original place and its relationships remain identifiable.
Build thick directional pigment into clouds, ground, foliage, and highlights, using selective
translucent glaze for light and water. Derive a vivid palette from the source and preserve
its luminous accents. Let scraped strokes reveal the paper grain within the painted forms.

At the scene's perimeter, let thick ridges taper into dragged pigment, broken dry scrapes and
scattered traces that expose the paper. Leave irregular paper channels between painted forms
and generous continuous paper around them. The paint island has an organic, unfinished outline,
never straight rectangular cutoffs or a pasted-on panel; retain sharp relief rather than blurring it.

It should feel touchable and self-contained, never a digital filter or plastic diorama.
If typography is requested, let a calligraphic title and small subordinate text occupy the
composition without obscuring its main subjects. Avoid muddy sepia, arbitrary desaturation, smooth
airbrush, stock dreamscapes unrelated to the source, generic figurines, fake signage,
and typography competing with the painted subject.
