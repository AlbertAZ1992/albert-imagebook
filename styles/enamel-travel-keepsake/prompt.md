# Enamel Keepsake · 珐琅拾光

Keep a memorable silhouette as a small enamel object with real thickness and a soft glint.
把照片里最值得记住的轮廓，收成一枚有厚度、有光泽的小小珐琅纪念物。

Keywords: Tiny keepsake · metal outlines · enamel sheen. 小型纪念物 · 金属轮廓 · 珐琅光泽。
Also known as: Enamel Travel Keepsake · 珐琅旅行纪念物.

## Prompt

Identify the single person, landmark, or landscape motif that carries the photograph's
memory. Compress it into a premium enamel magnet resting visually on exposed textured paper.
Derive its outer contour from that subject's strongest silhouette, keeping
one or two decisive identity cues and omitting windows, foliage, and cloud micro-detail.

Use a plausible manufacturable contour, narrow dark metal boundaries, and three to
six clean enamel color fields from the source. Retain its identifying warm-cool contrasts.
Restrained thickness, soft directional light, and a small contact shadow make it tangible.

Around the keepsake, use a sparse source-colored painted trace with dry-brush breaks and
pigment tails that disperse into exposed paper. Keep paper visible between these marks and
around the object, without a rectangular color field or enclosing frame. Preserve the enamel's
crisp metal silhouette; the surrounding painted marks soften and break apart, not the object.

The result feels like a collected memory rather than a catalog product photograph. An optional
short handwritten label may sit below the keepsake when wording is supplied or its subject
name is established. Avoid invented locations, generic shields, toy-like plastic borders,
chrome, glitter, photographic textures mapped onto the object, packaging, hands, props,
multiple products, and neutral tabletop scenes.
