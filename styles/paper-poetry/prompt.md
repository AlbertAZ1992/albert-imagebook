# Paper Poetry · 叠纸诗篇

Rebuild the photograph as thin layers of fibrous matte cardstock with gentle physical depth.
把照片重组成有纤维、有层次、有轻微投影的叠纸小诗。

Keywords: Layered cardstock · paper fibers · shallow shadows.
关键词：层叠卡纸 · 可见纤维 · 浅投影。
Also known as: Layered Paper · 纸艺叠层.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Rebuild the subject as thin layered matte cardstock with visible fibers, lifted layers and tiny
cast shadows. Use cream, dusty blue and muted coral. Preserve recognizable shapes while
simplifying detail. Keep open paper between the forms. No painted brush texture, thick sculpted
clay or low-poly 3D rendering.
