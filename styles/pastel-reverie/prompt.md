# Pastel Reverie · 粉彩微风

Rebuild the photograph with soft chalk, pencil edges and quiet color on open ivory paper.
让柔软粉彩和铅笔边缘轻轻留下照片里的动作、颜色与空气。

Keywords: Dry pastel · colored pencil · open paper. 干粉彩 · 彩铅边缘 · 露纸留白。
Also known as: Pastel Breeze · 粉彩轻风.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Use dry soft pastel and delicate colored-pencil edges on fine-grain ivory paper. Preserve
gestures and simplify incidental objects. Layer restrained peach, lavender, teal and pale gold.
Let chalk marks break into bare paper between and around forms. No thick paint, glossy rendering
or exaggerated cartoon proportions.
