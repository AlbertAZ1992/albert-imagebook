# Travel Stamps · 旅途印记

Distill a roofline, street, or shoreline into a small layered ink impression worth keeping.
把一座屋顶、一条街或一段海岸，印成旅行手账里值得收下的一枚彩色章印。

Keywords: Carved contours · imperfect registration · warm paper. 手刻轮廓 · 错位套印 · 温暖旧纸。
Also known as: Rubber Stamp Travel Journal · 橡皮章旅行手账.

## Prompt

Reduce the source place to the mark it might leave in a field journal. Build recognition
from its distinctive roof, arch, tower, facade, terrain, shoreline, vegetation, road,
or horizon relationships. Compress those contours into a multi-color rubber-stamp
impression on aged warm paper.

Print two to four restrained source-derived ink layers with carved marks, dry gaps,
uneven pressure, broken edges, and slight registration drift. Let the impression feel collected and observed rather than branded.

Let the peripheral ink marks thin into dry flecks, faint rubbed traces and missing impressions,
revealing continuous paper between layers and beyond the scene. Keep carved central contours
legible while the surrounding marks disperse naturally. Avoid a solid rectangular stamp bed,
an enclosing frame, clean-cut scene edges, and digital blur masquerading as worn ink.

A small field-note label may use facts supplied by the user or visibly established by
the image. When no new text is requested, simplify signs into unmarked shapes rather
than simulating letters. Avoid guessed place names or years, circular seals, postal
perforations, wax seals, stickers, generic city icons, repeated micro-detail, smooth
vector logos, and perfectly registered digital edges.
