# Arched Memory · 一扇海风

Keep the defining photographic subject inside an airy hand-drawn arch with watercolor accents.
把真实主体留在一扇轻盈的手绘拱窗里，让铅笔和水彩越过部分边缘。

Keywords: Photographic window · pencil arch · watercolor accents.
关键词：摄影窗口 · 铅笔拱形 · 水彩点染。
Also known as: Photo Arch · 记忆拱窗.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Keep the defining subject photographic inside a compact arched window, with a delicate incomplete
pencil arch and a few pale watercolor accents crossing selected edges. Preserve photo detail and
generous surrounding ivory paper. No unrelated flowers, place names, dates or captions.
