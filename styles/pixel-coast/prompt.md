# Pixel Coast · 像素假日

Reinterpret the photograph as a compact 16-bit micro-world with a gentle isometric viewpoint.
把照片变成一个紧凑的 16-bit 像素小世界，保留人物关系和场景辨识度。

Keywords: Square pixels · stepped silhouettes · restrained dithering.
关键词：方形像素 · 阶梯轮廓 · 克制抖色。
Also known as: 16-bit Holiday · 像素小世界.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Reinterpret the source as a compact 16-bit pixel-art micro-world with a gentle isometric
viewpoint. Use consistent square pixels, stepped silhouettes, restrained dithering and a limited
blue, seafoam, peach and sand palette. Retain key subjects and relationships. No blur, smooth
vector contours, game HUD or unrelated props.
