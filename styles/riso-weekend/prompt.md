# Riso Weekend · 孔版周末

Print the photograph as a loose three-ink risograph with overlaps and small registration shifts.
用三种油墨、网点和轻微错版，把照片印成一张松弛的周末孔版画。

Keywords: Three inks · halftone dots · registration drift.
关键词：三色油墨 · 网点 · 轻微错版。
Also known as: Risograph Weekend · 周末孔版画.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Use three risograph inks: teal, coral and ochre. Describe the subject with simplified shapes,
overlapping inks, subtle registration offsets and halftone dots. Leave clear paper openings.
Avoid full photographic tonal detail, smooth gradients and perfectly aligned digital color.
