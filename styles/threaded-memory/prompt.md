# Threaded Memory · 细线织忆

Translate the photograph into delicate silk-thread embroidery on continuous linen.
用细密丝线把照片里的轮廓、动作和颜色绣进一块连续的亚麻布。

Keywords: Silk thread · directional stitches · linen. 丝线 · 方向针脚 · 亚麻底。
Also known as: Embroidered Memory · 刺绣记忆.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Reconstruct the defining subject in fine silk-thread embroidery on continuous ivory linen,
viewed straight on. Satin and short directional stitches describe form with a subtle sheen.
Use sand, dusty blue, sage, peach and ochre. Preserve natural proportions; leave broad linen
openings. No hoop, patch border, chunky yarn or unrelated ornaments.
