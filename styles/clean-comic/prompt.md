# Clean Comic · 清爽漫画

Translate the photograph into a contemporary flat-color comic with natural proportions.
用清爽线条和平涂色块，把照片画成保留自然人物比例的当代漫画。

Keywords: Flat comic · elegant outlines · natural proportions.
关键词：平涂漫画 · 利落轮廓 · 自然比例。
Also known as: 海边漫画; Contemporary Comic · 当代漫画.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Use clean contemporary flat-color comic illustration with elegant dark outlines, simplified
natural adult proportions and recognizable clothing and gestures. Restrict colors to azure,
butter yellow and blush adapted to the source. Remove most environmental detail. No giant heads,
speech bubbles, realistic shading or painterly backgrounds.
