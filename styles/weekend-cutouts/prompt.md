# Weekend Cutouts · 周末剪贴

Keep the important photographic subjects and recompose them with a few quiet paper cutouts.
保留真实人物与物件，再用少量纸片把照片重新剪贴成一页周末记忆。

Keywords: Photo cutouts · muted paper shapes · crisp edges.
关键词：摄影剪影 · 柔和纸片 · 清晰切边。
Also known as: Photo Paper Collage · 照片纸艺剪贴.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Keep key subjects photographic and recompose their cutouts with at most a few muted blue and
apricot paper shapes. Preserve real texture and identity. Use crisp cut edges, occasional torn
paper and subtle contact shadows. Avoid reconstructing the entire rectangular photograph,
scrapbook clutter or unrelated decorations.
