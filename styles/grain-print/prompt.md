# Grain Print · 夏日套色

Turn the source into a joyful granular stencil print with visible missing ink and paper grain.
用明快套色、缺墨和纸面颗粒，把照片印成一张有夏天气息的版画。

Keywords: Stencil print · granular ink · bold shapes.
关键词：模版套色 · 油墨颗粒 · 大块造型。
Also known as: Summer Stencil Print · 夏日版画.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Use joyful granular stencil printing in cornflower blue, vermilion and leafy green on cream.
Describe the subject with chunky simplified shapes, missing ink and fine paper-grain speckles.
Keep broad unprinted openings. The texture should read as printmaking rather than oil strokes,
smooth vector fill or digital noise.
