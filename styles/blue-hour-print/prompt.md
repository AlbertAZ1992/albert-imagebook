# Blue Hour Print · 蓝晒时光

Reduce the photograph to one Prussian-blue cyanotype impression with handmade exposure edges.
把照片收进一层普鲁士蓝，让轮廓和光从手工蓝晒的边缘浮出来。

Keywords: Cyanotype · Prussian blue · negative highlights.
关键词：蓝晒 · 普鲁士蓝 · 负片高光。
Also known as: Cyanotype Memory · 蓝晒记忆.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Use a single Prussian blue cyanotype language on warm white paper: recognizable photographic
silhouettes, negative pale highlights and handmade exposure edges. Keep the impression compact
with ample surrounding ground. No other ink colors, polished vector contours or full-bleed blue.
