# Photo Play · 照片奇遇

Keep the real photographic subject and invite a few tiny drawn characters into its story.
让照片里的人和物保持真实，再邀请几个手绘小人进来，发生一点小小的奇遇。

Keywords: Real photo subjects · line characters · tiny humor. 真实照片主体 · 黑线小人 · 微型幽默。
Also known as: Photo Doodle Story · 照片涂鸦故事.

## Prompt

Read the photograph for a gesture, relationship, emotion, or small comic possibility.
Keep its essential subject photographic: preserve identity, body, clothing, texture,
and color. Isolate or recompose that real subject on warm near-white textured paper and let a
few tiny black-line figures participate in its story. Do not redraw the real subject
as a cartoon.

Choose one narrative idea. Let doodles observe, guide, follow, climb, carry, imitate, or
react to the subject. Add only the figures and props needed to express that interaction.
Use thin dark lines with natural wobble, restrained source-color accents, and a coherent relationship between the photographic and drawn elements. The figures feel like thoughtful marginalia, not decorative stickers.

Let retained environmental fragments and drawn color accents disperse through broken brushed
edges, sparse pigment and exposed paper. Keep that same paper visible between the photographic
and drawn elements and around the scene. Preserve the real subject's identity and anatomy;
let the surrounding marks trail off, without a rectangular photo patch or a uniform soft-focus halo.

The humor should come from a specific reading of this photograph. When text is requested,
use at most two short handwritten thoughts; omit wording that merely describes objects.
Avoid unrelated doodles, excessive props, dense handwriting, scrapbook collage,
infographics, advertising characters, generic inspirational quotes, and polished vectors.
