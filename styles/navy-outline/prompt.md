## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Use one deep navy ink with confident clean outline strokes, white interiors and a few solid blocks.
Simplify the key subject and gesture into one coherent die-cut sticker. Keep natural proportions.
Add a narrow white cut rim and a barely visible shadow. Remove photographic scenery and lettering;
retain only a few essential contextual lines. No hatching, painterly texture, gradients or extra
decoration.
