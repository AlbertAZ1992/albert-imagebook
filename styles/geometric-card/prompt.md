# Geometric Card · 几何卡纸

Reconstruct the photograph with sharply cut geometric cardstock planes and shallow shadows.
把照片拆成利落的几何卡纸，用层次、纤维和浅投影重新拼起来。

Keywords: Geometric cardstock · cut planes · shallow depth.
关键词：几何卡纸 · 切割色面 · 浅层立体。
Also known as: Cardstock Geometry · 几何纸艺.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Reconstruct the subject in sharply cut geometric cardstock planes. Preserve defining poses and
silhouettes, supported by a few source-related circles or stepped shapes. Use cobalt, sage, sand
and muted peach, fine fiber texture and shallow shadows. No photographic remnants, painterly
brushwork or low-poly 3D rendering.
