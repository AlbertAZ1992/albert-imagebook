# Summer Wordscape · 字里有海

Use an exact supplied word as the window for a coherent scene drawn from the photograph.
把用户给出的准确文字变成窗口，让照片里的故事长在字形里面。

Keywords: Letter mask · source scene · legible type.
关键词：字母蒙版 · 原图场景 · 清晰文字。
Also known as: Typographic Landscape · 字景.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Build a typographic travel artwork using the exact word supplied by the user. Fill large legible
block letters with a coherent source-inspired scene in muted teal, salmon and sand. Preserve the
most important subject across the letter composition; keep negative gaps readable. No invented
location names or secondary captions. If no word is supplied, ask for it before generation.
