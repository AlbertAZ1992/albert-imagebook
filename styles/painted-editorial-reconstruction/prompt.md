# Painterly Stories · 笔触叙事

Retell the movement and atmosphere of a photograph with a few expressive painted shapes.
用几笔有方向的颜色重讲照片里的动作和气氛，让画面像一页有故事的插画。

Keywords: Broad brushwork · color planes · movement. 宽笔触 · 大色面 · 动作与气氛。
Also known as: Painted Editorial Reconstruction · 大笔触绘画重构.

## Prompt

Rebuild the photograph's emotional structure with paint. Find one focal relationship,
movement, or directional rhythm and carry it through a few broad color planes, visible
matte brushwork and layered depth. Paint the focal relationship with conviction and allow
the environment to remain incomplete. Paper carries the light and air instead of a filled sky.
Keep the painted group compact and optically centered within a much larger sheet, with
generous quiet paper on both sides and balanced space above and below. A few strong strokes
tell the place; do not extend the horizon across the page or tilt the whole composition.

Keep the painting recognizable without tracing it. Remove repetitive background details
and group secondary forms into a few selective strokes separated by open paper. Leave generous
unpainted passages through the scene, not only around its outer edge. Avoid joining every
background form into a continuous painted backdrop. For skies and water, select a small
disconnected accent rather than a broad atmospheric wash behind all the subjects.
Individual gestures may be asymmetric while the whole group stays balanced and centered.
Change scale when it strengthens the reading without changing its identity. Let gouache,
acrylic, soft pastel, or restrained palette-knife texture create a calm editorial image.
Derive airy colors from the source, with a controlled accent when it helps the relationship.

Paint on visible textured paper. Let the broad peripheral strokes open into dry-brush gaps,
thin scumbles and fading pigment so the paper shows through both inside and around the scene.
Vary the broken edges with brush direction while preserving the focal subject. Do not fill a
rectangular backdrop, trim strokes at straight boundaries, or substitute a uniform blurred mask.

Optional editorial text is quiet, specific, and subordinate. Avoid literal full-scene
reproduction, photoreal painting, sticker collage, hard vector geometry, smooth airbrush,
plastic 3D, muddy filters, indiscriminate pastel washes, template titles, invented places,
and decoration competing with the central visual relationship.
