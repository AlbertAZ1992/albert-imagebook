# A Few Lines · 几笔之间

Find the smallest set of black pen lines that can still hold the photograph's defining gesture.
只留下最必要的几根黑线，也能让照片里的动作和关系被认出来。

Keywords: Black pen · broken contours · minimal detail.
关键词：黑色钢笔 · 断续轮廓 · 极简细节。
Also known as: Minimal Line Drawing · 极简线稿.

## Prompt

Read the source photo for its defining subject, pose and relationships. Preserve those facts.
Adapt the material and graphic language to the supplied scene; do not import motifs that do
not belong to the source.

Draw the subject with economical black pen contours and selectively broken lines. Use very little
hatching. Preserve defining gestures and spatial relationships; remove incidental detail. Leave
the surrounding paper open. No color, gray filled scenery or dense crosshatching.
